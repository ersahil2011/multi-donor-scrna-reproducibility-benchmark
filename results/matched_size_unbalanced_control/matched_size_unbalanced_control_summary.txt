INTERPRETATION
Both sampling designs contained exactly 6955 cells per iteration. Donor-balanced sampling produced higher ARI and NMI agreement with the full-data reference and recovered more clusters than proportional unbalanced sampling. Therefore, the balanced-analysis results cannot be explained only by reducing the total number of cells; donor representation also affected clustering preservation.
